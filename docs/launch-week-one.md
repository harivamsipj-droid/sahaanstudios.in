# Sahaan Studios — founder's first-week runbook

Last reviewed: 25 September 2026. This is an operating plan, not a record of interviews, verified artists, bookings or legal approval.

## Pilot decision

- Accept professional applications and customer enquiries from **all Hyderabad neighbourhoods**. Search results are public Google listings, not proof of Sahaan service coverage; confirm reviewed-artist availability for each PIN before offering a booking.
- Offer a managed **request**, not instant booking. No artist, time slot or price is confirmed until Sahaan checks and both sides accept in writing.
- Customer-facing services: gel polish, manicure, nail extensions and custom nail art. Start with a narrow, agreed treatment scope for each artist before activating them.
- Indicative starting guides: gel polish ₹599, manicure ₹999, extensions ₹1,399; custom nail art is quoted after a design brief. These are market benchmarks, not Sahaan commitments. The founder must validate artist costs, travel, materials, removal and taxes before issuing a final quote. Benchmark: [Urban Company Hyderabad nail menu](https://www.urbancompany.com/hyderabad-salon-nails), viewed 25 September 2026.

## Minimum artist activation checklist

1. Permission to use the artist's name, portfolio and service photographs.
2. Identity and contact checked through a secure process; do **not** ask for IDs or bank documents in an open WhatsApp chat.
3. Practical nail-work assessment and hygiene/clean-kit review completed.
4. Written service list, prices, exclusions, cancellation handling, travel radius and availability agreed.
5. Written commission and settlement terms agreed. The public founding offer currently promises 0% commission on the first 10 **completed** bookings, then a proposed 18% standard commission; honour any offer already made and cost it before scaling.
6. Only then mark the artist **Sahaan verified** or show them as available. A Google listing or rating is never evidence of Sahaan verification.

## Founder-run booking procedure

1. **Received:** WhatsApp enquiry arrives. Record requested service, PIN/area and preferred date; ask only for details needed to assess the request. Acknowledge that it is not confirmed.
2. **Coverage check:** Is a reviewed artist available for the requested PIN, service and time? If not, respond honestly and offer to follow up only with the customer's consent.
3. **Artist acceptance:** Share the relevant job details with one suitable artist. Confirm service scope, travel, materials, proposed time and their price before making an offer to the customer.
4. **Written quote:** Send the customer the artist's name or profile, treatment inclusions/exclusions, total price including travel and taxes where applicable, time, cancellation terms and payment method. Do not imply a Google-listed business has accepted.
5. **Confirmed:** Mark as confirmed only after the artist and customer both accept the same written details. Give the customer a booking reference.
6. **Delivery and follow-up:** Check arrival, completion, payment evidence and customer feedback. Record any complaint and resolution separately. Ask permission before using photos or testimonials.

## Private booking register — fields, not customer data

Keep the register in a restricted-access business system, not the public GitHub repository. Suggested fields: request ID; received date; source; customer contact; consent to follow up; PIN and area; requested service/date; artist ID; artist verification status; quote and travel fee; customer acceptance; artist acceptance; booking status; completion date; amount collected; commission basis; costs; complaint status; feedback; next action. Restrict access and agree a retention/deletion process with counsel.

Use statuses consistently: **new → qualifying → awaiting artist → quoted → confirmed → completed / cancelled**. A WhatsApp click or Google result is never a completed booking.

## Launch gate and weekly scorecard

- Each PIN/service combination stays enquiry-only until it has checked artist coverage and backup availability.
- Track enquiries, qualified requests, quotes, confirmed bookings, completed bookings, cancellations, response time, repeat customers, gross commission and direct support/acquisition costs.
- Revenue is collected only on completed, commission-paying work under agreed terms. The first 10 commission-free bookings per founding artist are a real acquisition cost, not revenue.
- Do not launch paid ads at scale until a completed-booking contribution calculation is positive or repeat bookings clearly justify the acquisition cost.

## Decisions still requiring founder/counsel approval

- Final treatment inclusions and Sahaan selling prices after artist quotes; the website guide is not a fixed price list.
- Exact PIN/service coverage and number of checked artists available for each area.
- Cancellation/refund policy, deposit amount if any, payment processor and who invoices the customer. The current website does **not** collect payment.
- Indian GST/e-commerce-operator treatment, professional agreements and customer-data practices before paid transactions or ID/bank-document collection.
